About this service

Prototype Safety Check is an official passive examination tool for deployed prototypes. It performs read-only checks and never attempts exploitation.

Scope of examination

  • Transport security and encryption configuration
  • Injection, scripting & content-security posture
  • Malware indicators & phishing resemblance

Legal notice

Use of this service constitutes confirmation that you own each submitted website or hold the owner's written permission. Results are advisory and do not certify absolute security.
